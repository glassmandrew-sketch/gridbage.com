Cribbage. Reimagined.

GRIDBAGE takes the classic card game you love and puts it on a diagonal grid. Place your cards strategically, outmaneuver a ruthless AI, and rack up cribbage points across your scoring lanes. Every hand is a fresh puzzle.

How to Play

Four things to know before you deal your first hand

🃏
The Board
THE GRIDBAGE BOARD

Place your 5 cards across the diagonal board to score the most cribbage points.

The center card — the Turn — is shared by everyone and counts toward every lane.

🎯
Your Scoring Lanes
ROWS
COLUMNS

Each game you're assigned Rows or Columns. Your 3 gold lanes are yours to score.

Gold outlines show your lanes at the start of each game. The AI scores the opposite.

DISCARD 2 CARDS BEFORE PLAY

Before play begins, each player discards 2 cards to the Crib — a secret bonus hand scored by the dealer at the end of the round.

You take turns dealing so the Crib advantage alternates every game.
