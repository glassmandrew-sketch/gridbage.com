Gridbage is a bold reinvention of the classic card game cribbage — blending the deep strategy of hold'em poker with the beloved scoring traditions of cribbage. Every placement is a decision. Every card you hold is a weapon.

How It Works

Simple to learn. A lifetime to master.

1

Get Dealt 8 Cards

Both players receive 8 cards. Choose 2 to discard into the crib — a bonus hand scored at the end.

2

Build the Grid

Take turns placing your remaining 6 cards onto a unique triangular grid board. Every move blocks your opponent while setting up your own lines.

3

Reveal the Cut

The cut card is revealed. Every row and column scores like cribbage — fifteens, pairs, runs, flushes, nobs. The center card is shared by all.

4

Race to 121

Watch your pegs race around the classic oval cribbage board. First to 121 wins. Skunks and double skunks count.
