The Game

Classic Cribbage, Beautifully Played

Duplicate Card Cribbage brings the full classic cribbage experience to your iPhone — clean, fast, and faithful to the rules you know and love.

01

Deal & Discard

Each player receives 6 cards and discards 2 into the crib. The crib alternates between players each hand.

02

Cut the Starter

A starter card is revealed. If it's a Jack — His Heels — the dealer immediately scores 2 points.

03

Pegging

Players alternate playing cards, scoring along the way. Count up to 31 for points — 15s, pairs, runs, and Go.

04

Count Hands

Score your hand, then the AI's hand, then the crib. Non-dealer counts first. First to 121 wins.

Duplicate Mode

Remove Luck. Reward Skill.

In Duplicate Mode, 9 hands are pre-dealt before the game begins. Every hand you play, the AI plays with the same cards. The luck of the draw is the same for both sides — only your decisions determine the winner.

It's the purest test of cribbage skill on your iPhone.
