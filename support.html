DISCARD 2 CARDS BEFORE PLAY

Before play begins, each player sends 2 cards to the Crib — a secret bonus hand.

The dealer scores the Crib after the round. You take turns dealing, so the advantage alternates.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why are the red 5s crossed out?

The red 5s (5♥ and 5♦) are removed from the deck as part of GRIDBAGE's house rules. This is a deliberate design choice to balance gameplay — 5s are unusually powerful in cribbage scoring and their removal creates a more strategic experience.

What is a skunk — and what's a double skunk?

Skunks are penalty multipliers applied when you lose by a large margin. You must cross each line to avoid them:

🏆91–120 ptsRegular loss — one game lost
🦨61–90 ptsSkunk — counts as TWO losses
🦨🦨0–60 ptsDouble skunk — counts as THREE losses

My stats reset — what happened?

Stats are stored locally on your device. If you deleted and reinstalled the app, or restored from a backup, your stats may have been lost. Unfortunately we cannot recover local stats remotely. Email us if you're having ongoing issues.

How do I report a bug?

Email us at support@gridbage.com with a description of what happened, what you expected to happen, and your iOS version. Screenshots are always helpful!
