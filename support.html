Frequently asked questions and how to get help.

Gameplay

How does Gridbage scoring work?+
Gridbage uses standard cribbage scoring. Every row and column on the triangular grid is scored as a cribbage hand — fifteens (2 pts each), pairs (2 pts), runs (1 pt per card), flushes (1 pt per card), and nobs (1 pt for a Jack matching the cut suit). The center card is shared by every line that passes through it. Crib scoring follows the same rules, and the dealer's crib is scored at the end of each round.
What is the crib and how does it work?+
Each round, both players discard 2 cards from their 8-card hand into the crib. The crib is a bonus hand scored by the dealer at the end of the round using standard cribbage scoring, including the cut card. Choosing what to put into the crib — especially when your opponent is the dealer — is one of the deepest strategic decisions in Gridbage.
What is the cut card?+
After both players discard to the crib and before placement begins, a cut card is drawn from the remaining deck. This card contributes to the scoring of every row, column, and the crib — just like in traditional cribbage. If the cut card is a Jack, the dealer scores 2 points immediately ("His Heels").
What are skunks and double skunks?+
A skunk occurs when the winner crosses 121 while the loser hasn't passed 91. A double skunk is when the loser hasn't passed 61. Both are tracked in your stats.

Difficulty Levels

What's the difference between difficulty levels?+
🌱 Beginner — "I know a little cribbage..." The AI makes deliberate mistakes and gifts scoring cards into the crib. A forgiving way to learn.

⚔️ Intermediate — "I picked a bad week to...stop playing beginner." The AI plays optimally half the time and gifts the other half.

💀 Expert — "To the Pain..." The AI seem like it reads your mind, plans ahead, and plays to win. No gifts. No mercy.

🚀 Ludicrous Speed — "Surely you can't be serious." The AI starts learning your game patterns and uses it for every decision. Pure computational carnage.
Can I change difficulty mid-game?+
Difficulty is set at the start of each game and cannot be changed during play. Start a New Game from the menu to choose a different level.

Duplicate Card Mode

What is Duplicate Card Mode?+
Duplicate Card Mode eliminates luck by having both you and the AI play the exact same cards. You play one round with your hand, then swap and play the AI's hand. A full comparison breakdown shows who scored better with each set of cards — pure skill, no excuses.

App & Technical

Does Gridbage require an internet connection?+
No. Gridbage works completely offline. There's no account, no login, no server communication of any kind. Play anywhere, anytime.
My stats were lost — can I recover them?+
Stats are stored locally on your device only. If you deleted and reinstalled the app, restored from a backup, or reset your device, the stats may have been lost. Unfortunately we cannot recover local stats remotely since we never have access to them — that's by design for your privacy.
How do I report a bug?+
Email us at support@gridbage.com with a description of what happened, what device and iOS version you're on, and any steps to reproduce. Screenshots always help. We read and respond to every message.
Does Gridbage collect any of my data?+
No. Zero. Nothing is collected, transmitted, or stored outside your device. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers. See our full Privacy Policy for details.
