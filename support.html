Frequently Asked Questions

Gameplay

How do I score points in Gridbage?+
Each row and column on the grid is scored like a cribbage hand — fifteens (2 pts each), pairs (2 pts), runs (1 pt per card), flushes (4 or 5 pts), and nobs (1 pt if your Jack matches the cut card's suit). The center card is shared by all rows and columns. After all cards are placed and the cut card is revealed, every complete line scores automatically.
What is the crib?+
The crib is a bonus hand scored at the end of each round. Each player discards 2 cards into it before placing cards on the grid. The dealer scores the crib — so strategy includes choosing which cards to throw in (or keep out of) the crib based on who the dealer is.
What is the cut card?+
The cut card (also called the "starter") is drawn from the deck at the end of the hand and shared by both players. It adds to the scoring of every line on the grid and to the crib. In Ludicrous Speed mode, the AI knows the cut card before the game even begins — and uses that information for every decision it makes.
What is a skunk?+
A skunk occurs when the winning player reaches 121 before the loser reaches 91 points. A double skunk is when the loser hasn't even reached 61. In tournament cribbage, skunks and double skunks count as 2× and 3× wins respectively. Gridbage tracks both for your statistics.
What is Duplicate Card mode?+
Duplicate Card mode deals the same cards to both you and the AI across matched sets — you each play the other's hand in the next round. At the end, a full comparison table shows each hand's results side-by-side with a grid-points edge differential. It's designed to eliminate luck and measure pure decision-making skill.

Difficulty Levels

What's the difference between difficulty levels?+
🌱 Beginner — "I know a little cribbage..." The AI makes strategic mistakes and intentionally tosses you useful cards (pairs, connectors, fives) in your crib.

⚔️ Intermediate — "I picked a bad week to...stop playing beginner." The AI plays optimally half the time and gifts the other half.

💀 Expert — "To the Pain..." Full beam search, hand reading, defensive blocking, pocket mobility, and crib optimization. No gifts. Ever.

🚀 Ludicrous Speed — "Surely you can't be serious." The AI peeks at the cut card before crib selection and uses exact cut card scoring for every placement decision throughout the game.
Can I change difficulty mid-game?+
Difficulty is set at the start of each game and cannot be changed during play. Start a New Game from the menu to choose a different level.

App & Technical

Does Gridbage require an internet connection?+
No. Gridbage works completely offline. There's no account, no login, no server communication of any kind. Play anywhere, anytime.
My stats were lost — can I recover them?+
Stats are stored locally on your device only. If you deleted and reinstalled the app, restored from a backup, or reset your device, the stats may have been lost. Unfortunately we cannot recover local stats remotely since we never have access to them — that's by design for your privacy.
How do I report a bug?+
Email us at support@gridbage.com with a description of what happened, what device and iOS version you're on, and any steps to reproduce. Screenshots always help. We read and respond to every message.
Does Gridbage collect any of my data?+
No. Zero. Nothing is collected, transmitted, or stored outside your device. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers. See our full Privacy Policy for details.
