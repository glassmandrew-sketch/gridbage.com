📖 How to Play

🃏
The Board is your battle GRID!
THE GRIDBAGE BOARD

Each player is dealt 8 cards, 2 for the Crib, 5 for the GRID and one HOLD card.

The center card — the TURN card — is shared and counts toward both the center row and center column.

DISCARD 2 CARDS BEFORE PLAY

Before play begins, each player discards 2 cards to the Crib — an extra bonus hand scored by the dealer at the end of the round by using the 4 crib cards and the CUT card.

You take turns dealing so the Crib advantage alternates every game.

📋
A Few Extra GRIDBAGE Rules
🃏

The dealer places the first card onto the GRID after the TURN card is shown.

🔢

Hands are counted left to right and top to bottom. The non-dealer counts one lane first, then the dealer counts their first lane and their Crib, then players alternate counting their remaining lanes until done.

🂠

You play with 50 cards — the 5♥ and 5♦ are removed from the deck.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why are the red 5s removed?

The red 5s (5♥ and 5♦) are removed from the deck as part of GRIDBAGE's house rules. This is a deliberate design choice to balance gameplay — 5s are unusually powerful in cribbage scoring and their removal creates a more strategic experience.

What is a skunk — and what's a double skunk?

Skunks are penalty multipliers applied when you lose by a large margin. You must cross each line to avoid them:

L
91–120 ptsRegular loss — one game lost
🦨
61–90 ptsSkunk — counts as TWO losses
🦨🦨
0–60 ptsDouble skunk — counts as THREE losses

My stats reset — what happened?

Stats are stored locally on your device. If you deleted and reinstalled the app, or restored from a backup, your stats may have been lost. Unfortunately we cannot recover local stats remotely. Email us if you're having ongoing issues.

How do I report a bug?

Email us at [email protected] with a description of what happened, what you expected to happen, and your iOS or Android version. Screenshots are always helpful!
