Frequently Asked Questions

Gameplay

How do I score points in Gridbage?+
Each row and column on the triangular grid is scored like a cribbage hand — fifteens (2 pts each), pairs (2 pts), runs (1 pt per card in the run), flushes (4 or 5 pts), and nobs (1 pt if your Jack matches the cut card's suit). The center card is shared by all rows and columns. After all cards are placed and the cut card is revealed, every complete line scores automatically.
What is the crib?+
The crib is a bonus hand scored at the end of each round. Each player discards 2 cards into it before placing cards on the grid. The dealer scores the crib — so strategy includes choosing which cards to throw in (or keep out of) the crib based on who the dealer is. The dealer alternates each game.
What is the cut card?+
The cut card (also called the "starter") is drawn from the deck after all cards are placed and shared by both players. It scores in every line on the grid and in the crib. In Ludicrous Speed mode, the AI knows the cut card before the game even begins — and uses that information for every decision it makes.
What is a skunk — and a double skunk?+
A skunk occurs when the winning player reaches 121 before the loser reaches 91. A double skunk is when the loser hasn't reached 61. In tournament cribbage, these count as extra losses. Gridbage tracks both for your statistics.
🏆91 – 120 ptsRegular loss
🦨61 – 90 ptsSkunk — counts as 2 losses
🦨🦨0 – 60 ptsDouble skunk — counts as 3 losses
What is Duplicate Card mode?+
Duplicate Card mode deals the same cards to both you and the AI across matched sets — you each play the other's hand. At the end, a full comparison table shows each hand's results side-by-side with a score differential. It's designed to eliminate luck entirely and measure pure decision-making skill. No lucky draws. No excuses.

Difficulty Levels

What's the difference between difficulty levels?+
🌱 Beginner — "I know a little cribbage..." The AI makes strategic mistakes and intentionally tosses useful cards (pairs, connectors, fives) into your crib.

⚔️ Intermediate — "I picked a bad week to...stop playing beginner." The AI plays optimally half the time and gifts you the other half.

💀 Expert — "To the Pain..." The AI seems like it reads your mind, plans ahead, and plays to win. No gifts. No mercy.

🚀 Ludicrous Speed — "Surely you can't be serious." The AI starts learning your game patterns and uses them for every decision. Pure computational carnage.
Can I change difficulty mid-game?+
Difficulty is set at the start of each game and cannot be changed during play. Start a New Game from the menu to choose a different level.

App & Technical

Does Gridbage require an internet connection?+
No. Gridbage works completely offline. There's no account, no login, no server communication of any kind. Play on a plane, in the woods, anywhere.
My stats were lost — can I recover them?+
Stats are stored locally on your device only. If you deleted and reinstalled the app, restored from a backup, or reset your device, the stats may have been lost. Unfortunately, we cannot recover local stats remotely since we never have access to them — that's by design for your privacy.
How do I report a bug?+
Email us at support@gridbage.com with a description of what happened, what device and software version you're on, and any steps to reproduce. Screenshots always help. We read and respond to every message.
Does Gridbage collect any of my data?+
No. Zero. Nothing is collected, transmitted, or stored outside your device. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers. See our full Privacy Policy for details.
