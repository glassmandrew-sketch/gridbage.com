Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

🔒 The short version: GRIDBAGE does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Ever.

Overview

GRIDBAGE is a single-player card game app developed for iOS. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is and is not collected when you use the app.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. GRIDBAGE does not require an account, login, or any personal details to play.

All game data — including your win/loss record, scores, and statistics — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Data We Do Not Collect

GRIDBAGE does not collect or transmit:

— Your name, email address, or contact information

— Location data

— Device identifiers or advertising IDs

— Usage analytics or behavioral data

— Payment or financial information

Third-Party Services

GRIDBAGE does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services. We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data to share.

Children's Privacy

GRIDBAGE does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app contains no data collection of any kind.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, we will revise the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
