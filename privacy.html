Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 26, 2026

✓ Zero data collected · Zero tracking · Zero ads

About Gridbage

Gridbage is a strategic card game developed and published by GLC Property LLC. For more information, visit www.gridbage.com.

Information We Collect

Gridbage does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. All game data — including your scores, statistics, and game history — is stored exclusively on your device and never leaves it.

Data Storage

Game statistics (wins, losses, scores) are saved locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data is never uploaded to any server, never shared with third parties, and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app or reset your stats from within the app.

No Analytics or Tracking

Gridbage does not use any analytics services, advertising SDKs, crash reporting tools, or tracking technologies. We do not track your location, device identifiers, or usage behavior in any form.

No Account Required

Gridbage requires no account, login, email address, or registration of any kind. You can play the game entirely and permanently anonymously.

No Ads

Gridbage contains no advertisements. We do not work with ad networks or allow advertisers to access any information through our app — because there is no information to access.

Children's Privacy

Gridbage does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since we collect no personal data from any user, the app is safe for all ages. We comply fully with COPPA and similar regulations worldwide.

Third-Party Services

Gridbage does not integrate any third-party services, social platforms, payment processors, or external SDKs that collect user data.

Changes to This Policy

Any future updates to this privacy policy will be reflected in the app and at www.gridbage.com/privacy.html. We will update the effective date at the top of this page when changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Visit us at www.gridbage.com or email support@gridbage.com.
