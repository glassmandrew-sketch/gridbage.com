Privacy Policy

Effective February 26, 2026 · GLC Property LLC

🔒 Zero Data Collected

Overview

Gridbage is designed with one privacy principle: we collect nothing. No personal data, no usage analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no crash reports are transmitted from your device. Ever.

Data We Collect

None. Gridbage does not collect, transmit, store, or process any data from your device. Specifically:

  • No personal information (name, email, phone, location)
  • No device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, fingerprints)
  • No usage or behavioral analytics
  • No crash reports or diagnostics
  • No advertising or tracking identifiers
  • No cookies, pixels, or third-party SDKs

Local Storage

Game statistics (win/loss records, scores, skunks) are stored locally on your device using standard on-device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. If you delete the app, this data is permanently removed.

Third-Party Services

Gridbage uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing services. There are no SDKs, frameworks, or libraries in the app that transmit data off your device.

Children's Privacy

Because Gridbage collects no data of any kind, it is inherently compliant with COPPA and all children's privacy regulations. No special data practices apply because there is no data collection.

Internet Access

Gridbage does not require or use an internet connection. The app functions entirely offline. No network requests are made at any time during use.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices, we will update this page. Given that our current practice is to collect nothing, any change would be announced prominently in the app and on this page before taking effect.

Contact

Questions or concerns about this privacy policy? Contact us at support@gridbage.com.
