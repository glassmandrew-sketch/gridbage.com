Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026

🔒

Your Privacy in Plain English

Gridbage collects nothing. No data is gathered, stored, transmitted, or shared — ever. There is no account, no login, no server, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. Gridbage does not request access to your name, email address, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or any other personal data.

The app does not include any analytics SDKs, crash reporting tools, advertising networks, or any other third-party data collection frameworks.

Game Data & Statistics

All game statistics (wins, losses, scores, skunks) are stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or anyone else.

If you delete the app or restore your device from a backup, this locally stored data may be lost. We have no ability to recover it remotely.

Third-Party Services

Gridbage does not integrate with any third-party services. There are no advertising networks, no analytics platforms, no social login integrations, and no external API calls of any kind.

Children's Privacy

Gridbage is rated 4+ and is appropriate for all ages. Because we collect no data from any users, the app fully complies with COPPA and all applicable children's privacy regulations.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Given the nature of the app — zero data collection — meaningful changes are unlikely. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Email us at support@gridbage.com or visit www.gridbage.com.
